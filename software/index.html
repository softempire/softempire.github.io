MCapture Screen recorder · Windows

A lightweight screen recorder for Windows with a familiar, no-fuss interface. MCapture drives FFmpeg directly, so there are no capture drivers or background services. Pick an area, press F12, and record.

Version1.0.43
PlatformWindows 10 / 11
Size103 MB
FormatInstaller (.zip)

Download MCapture 1.0.43unzip, then run MCapture-Setup-1.0.43.exe

Highlights

01 · Capture

Any area, any way

Select an area, fixed rectangle, full screen, a single window, or a region that follows the mouse. Audio-only mode too.

02 · Audio

System sound and mic

Record speakers and microphone with separate volume control, mixed into one AAC track.

03 · Output

MP4, MKV or GIF

H.264 / HEVC with hardware encoders (NVENC, Quick Sync, AMF), plus pause and resume without re-encoding.

04 · Extras

Overlays and screenshots

Webcam, watermark and click effects, global hotkeys, and PNG / JPG / BMP screenshots.
